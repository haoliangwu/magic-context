# Magic Context concurrency limits (CK concurrency spec §3)

This is the Magic Context contribution to the [CK-wide concurrency spec](../../../subconscious/docs/specs/ck-concurrency.md). Paths and line numbers below are relative to this repository at the writing revision; a shortened filename in a citation refers to the unique fully qualified filename cited earlier. `inline` means the implementation has a literal or expression rather than a named constant. A per-attempt timeout is **not** a total pass, fallback-chain, or owned-background-work deadline. The fleet rules (§0) require caller-bounded retries, no-effect-only retry, terminal unknown refusals, per-connection/target admission and reasoned refusals; §2 requires outer deadlines to exceed inner work and the module drain window to exceed admitted requests.

## Rust `ck-mc` admission and lifecycle

`ck-mc` advertises `Concurrency::ModuleManaged` (`crates/mc-module/src/lib.rs:14815-14853`); dispatch has no general per-target request queue or in-flight cap (`lib.rs:14773-14797,14815-14853`). Historian guards are per session, while `MAX_ACTIVE_SNAPSHOT_LEASES = 8` is a wrapup-specific bound, not general request admission (`lib.rs:1322-1328,5775-5831,19673-19684`). `route.open` to management targets (`DEFAULT_THALAMUS_MODULE_ID`, `DEFAULT_RUNNER_MODULE_ID`) is sent once in the Broca path; host runner omits Broca (`crates/mc-module/src/route_targets.rs:14-16,43-76`; `historian_producer.rs:987-1041`). Session resolution has `SESSION_RESOLVE_DEADLINE = 2 s` and route retry `max_attempts = 1` (`crates/mc-module/src/session_resolver.rs:19,161-192`).

Store opening starts on `HELLO_ACK`: `STORE_LEASE_WAIT_WINDOW = 60 s`, `STORE_LEASE_INITIAL_BACKOFF = 250 ms`, `STORE_LEASE_MAX_BACKOFF = 1 s`; each arriving request waits only `STORE_OPENING_REQUEST_WAIT = 500 ms` before a reasoned `store_opening`/`store_lease_wait` refusal, or `store_open_failed` after expiry (`crates/mc-module/src/lib.rs:248-283,645-705,4521-4742`). The open can continue *after* that request refuses. The module has no local `module_warming` response/readiness branch: health while waiting reports degraded and request status names storage state; daemon route readiness is delegated to the serving SDK (`lib.rs:722-792,10260-10294,14801-14812`). The module's `TRANSFORM_HISTORIAN_FOLLOWUP_BUDGET = 20 s` bounds waiting plus inline historian follow-up, not the spawned historian's full lifetime (`lib.rs:245-247,7170-7226`).

## §0/§2 comparison — current gaps and fix direction

These are current behavior, not proposed new limits. The daemon's 12 s bind relay and default 30 s drain, SDK's 30 s default call timeout (TS response wait only), and 90 s route-open retry window are **outside** this module (§1–2 of fleet spec). Even a 15 s request exceeds 12 s if performed synchronously in `on_bind`; normal requests here are dispatched after bind, so do not conflate those clocks. The 45–90 s transform, 600 s historian, and 20-minute Dreamer work exceed the default drain unless detached/durable work is given an independent owner and shutdown rule. A 15 s boot registration race does not bound a 30 s migration retry ladder.

| Gap against fleet rule | Fix direction (not implemented) |
| --- | --- |
| Transform has no whole-pass deadline: 15 s initial + 45 s final-page completion, multiple 5 s pages, and a *fresh* 45–90 s `need_full_sync` resend can outlive a caller; the module-only fold can be admitted with 15 s and retry after the first attempt while work is still underway (`rust-mode-transform.ts:2018-2055,3461-3513`). | Carry one caller deadline through uploads, execution and resends; use a larger pre-admission fold budget or return a durable same-request handle, and never restart a possibly effective mutation. |
| Boot's 15 s race leaves an uncancelled migration opener with up to 30 s lock-retry sleeps, plus process probes and SQLite waits (`storage-gate.ts:136-150`; `migrations.ts:32-49,3366-3400`). | Cancel or explicitly own/de-duplicate the opener with its own stated total budget and reasoned degraded status; clamp its retries to that budget. |
| Historian TypeScript fallback/repair attempts each reset a 600 s timer, while the foreground may stop waiting at 60 s. Rust Broca's connect/route/subscription setup lies outside its await timer, and timeout/uncertain-status recovery can advance to another model despite unconfirmed cancellation (`compartment-runner-historian.ts:583-705,812-894`; `transform-compartment-phase.ts:322-345`; `crates/mc-module/src/historian_producer.rs:710-741,951-967`; `historian.rs:2309-2493,2909-2971`). | Set a whole-chain deadline and propagate remaining time; require terminal/no-effect evidence before a new model send, otherwise reattach or finish unknown. State a durable, bounded post-response historian budget. |
| Rust `unknown_module` wrapup is retried after 30 s; producer error classification also treats unrecognized text containing “retry”/“transient” as retryable (`crates/mc-module/src/lib.rs:7265-7275`; `historian_producer.rs:491-540`). | Make unknown codes terminal, and retry a known refusal only on pre-effect evidence within the original caller deadline. |
| Synapse retries `outcome_unknown`, maps unknown codes to transient, replays `embed.batch` on restart and accepts uncapped `retry_after_ms`; slice retries can resend a definitive empty/400 outcome (`embedding-synapse.ts:277-301,834-933,1085-1127`; `project-embedding-registry.ts:2897-2975`). | Use a documented no-effect code allowlist and idempotency result lookup for uncertain effects; cap backoff by the enclosing search/background-job deadline and stop on unknown codes. |
| Channel 2 restores `claimed → pending` after any send exception and a claim expiry can rearm an actually delivered but unconfirmed message; some event sends are unawaited (`channel2-delivery.ts:139-170,246-329`; `event-handler.ts:168-193,967-989`; `packages/pi-plugin/src/ctx-reduce-nudge-pi.ts:281-338`). | Keep uncertain delivery terminal/pending reconciliation, use a durable delivery identity and acknowledgement before rearm; bound and own event tasks per session. |
| Detached memory embeddings have no queue/in-flight cap; shadow embedding ticks reschedule an uncapped queue and each awaited item may exceed the 2 s tick budget. Historian can outlive the transform join; Dreamer has per-task time but no cumulative token ceiling and may fall back to synchronous child prompting (`ctx-memory/tools.ts:742-778`; `project-embedding-registry.ts:2189-2244`; `compartment-runner.ts:171-208`; `dreamer/task-executor.ts:417-430`; `prompt-async-transport.ts:41-65`). | Assign durable/deduplicated per-target queues, explicit total wall/token budgets and shutdown ownership; propagate cancellation into awaited providers and child sessions. |
| No general per-target `ck-mc` request cap under `ModuleManaged`; store lease acquisition runs up to 60 s after a caller gets a 500 ms refusal, and the module does not surface `module_warming` while opening (`crates/mc-module/src/lib.rs:248-283,645-705,14815-14853`). | Declare bounded per-target admission/queue and typed capacity versus warm-up versus permission refusals; expose warming readiness, and own/cancel store open with a stated background budget. |
| The foreground write-lock retry blocks the host's event loop: each `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` attempt waits synchronously up to the 5 s `busy_timeout`, and the backoff between attempts is a synchronous `Atomics.wait`, so one acquisition can hold OpenCode's main thread for about 16.5 s. With a write lock held elsewhere, OpenCode 2 stops answering `/health` (reproduced with 11 hosts, `docs/reports/issue-554-multi-host-contention.md`; `shared/sqlite.ts:490-562`, `v2/hooks/context.ts:1122-1127`). | In progress: short synchronous attempts with an asynchronous backoff inside the same total budget and outcome; background writers try once briefly and defer; acquisition waits logged by site. |

Other potential limit exposures: `ctx_search`, `ctx_note` and `ctx_memory` accept unbounded positive result limits; `ctx_expand message=N` bypasses the range token budget (`ctx-search/tools.ts:38-44`; `ctx-note/tools.ts:520-545`; `ctx-memory/tools.ts:224-285`; `ctx-expand/render.ts:181-245`). Clamp them per request/target; they are output-work bounds rather than proof of a retry violation. Dreamer scheduled retries retain the same due time for transient failure, so repeated ticks can reattempt without a separate retry backoff (`dreamer/task-scheduler.ts:291-342`); carry an explicit next-attempt time and stop at the task's caller/owned-job deadline.
